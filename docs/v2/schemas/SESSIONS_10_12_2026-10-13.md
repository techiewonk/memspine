# Schema sessions 10-12: read and cache, erasure and retention, plug-ins (owner, 2026-10-13)

These are sessions 10, 11 and 12 of `SCHEMA_AGENDA.md`. The owner took the recommended option each time. With these
three answered, every session on the agenda is now decided.

## Session 10: read and cache (ER-24, ER-29, ER-31, POL-1)

- **Evidence bundle.** A bundle is an assertion together with its support evidence, its binding evidence and its time
  anchors. A bundle goes into the context whole or not at all.
- **Budget accounting.** The budget is counted with the reader model's exact tokenizer, including headers and reserves.
  If no tokenizer is available, the count is marked `accounting=estimated`.
- **Viewer.** A multi-party read must carry a viewer bound to the authenticated principal. A read without one is
  rejected.
- **Cache key.** A cache entry is keyed on these fields:
  - scope
  - viewer
  - normalised query
  - config and flag hash
  - model ids
  - store generation
  - erasure generation

  Caches stay off until the ER-31 tests pass.
- **Revalidation.** Every result is revalidated for visibility and erasure immediately before it is returned (POL-1).
- **Order of read steps.** The read runs lift, re-admit, dedupe, cap, then rerank (ER-21). Results that were not scored
  are marked unscored, never given a score of 0.

## Session 11: erasure and retention (ER-27, ER-30)

- **What happens at erasure.** The erased data becomes logically invisible immediately. An erasure manifest is kept: it
  holds the ids and the erasure generation, and no content.
- **Physical purge.** Projections, vectors, graph and caches are purged physically by a tracked job that reports its
  progress.
- **Restoring a backup.** Restoring a backup re-applies the erasure manifest.
- **Rolling retention.** Under rolling retention, lineage and tombstones are kept, and the store reports that only a
  limited rebuild is possible.

## Session 12: plug-in manifest (ER-34)

- **Enablement.** Entry points are discovered, but a plug-in is used only if it is listed in the config under
  `plugins:` with its name and a version pin. A hash pin is optional.
- **Duplicates.** Two providers for the same slot cause an error at startup.
- **Lifetime.** Each plug-in declares its lifetime (`app`, `job` or `request`), and the DI container checks it.
- **Run manifest.** The list of enabled plug-ins is recorded in the run manifest (P0-03).
