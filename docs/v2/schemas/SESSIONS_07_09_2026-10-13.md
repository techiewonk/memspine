# Schema sessions 7-9: receipt, jobs, overload (owner, 2026-10-13)

These are sessions 7, 8 and 9 of `SCHEMA_AGENDA.md`. The owner took the recommended option each time.

## Session 7: receipt and readiness (ER-06 A)
- **Receipt.** The receipt reports durable acceptance: `durability=durable`, or `memory` in ephemeral mode, which carries the weaker guarantee.
- **Readiness.** It also gives readiness per capability. The capabilities are `raw_search`, `facts`, `graph`, `summaries` and `profile`, and each one is `pending`, `ready`, `unavailable` or `failed`.
- **Unconfigured capabilities.** A capability that is not configured reports `unavailable`. The receipt never claims `pending` for it.
- **Waiting.** `wait(capabilities, timeout)` returns the readiness reached so far. A read issued after a timed-out wait is marked `partial`, with the missing capabilities listed.
- **Retries.** A retry, as defined by the S3-3 write id, returns the original receipt.

## Session 8: jobs and progress (ER-07, ER-09, ER-10, ER-26, OF-1)
- **Leases.** Each claim takes a lease with a strictly increasing `epoch`. A commit is conditional on the epoch still being current, so a stale worker's commit is refused and logged.
- **Progress.** Progress is a contiguous watermark plus an explicit list of holes. Each hole records `(offset, reason, attempts, last_error)`. Holes are retried within the ER-15 budget, and they stay visible until they are filled or the owner waives them.
- **Job input.** A job's input is an immutable snapshot that names the target offset. Triggers that arrive while a job runs coalesce into one follow-up job. They never mutate the running job.
- **Handles.** Handle states are `queued`, `running`, `succeeded`, `failed`, `cancelled` and `coalesced`. `sleep()` returns a handle and does not block (OF-1).
- **Shutdown.** Unfinished jobs persist across `stop()`. Stop drains for a bounded time, then records the remaining jobs as queued. Fixing `windows.py:568-569` is part of this.

## Session 9: overload and accounting (ER-08, ER-15, ER-39)
- **Quotas.** Quotas cover storage, queue depth and rate, and they are checked before acceptance.
- **Rejection.** A write over quota is rejected with a typed `QuotaExceeded(quota, limit, current, retry_after)`. Rejected writes produce no receipt.
- **Accepted writes are kept.** An accepted write is never dropped later. If derivation cannot keep up, its jobs wait in the queue, and when the recovery budget runs out they become holes.
- **Recovery budget.** The budget is set per source, in model calls and tokens, and it is spent separately for each purpose: extraction, recovery and coverage.
- **Cost classes.** The classes are `generative`, `embedding`, `rerank` and `tool`, recorded per request and per job. Generative calls are counted separately from embedding and rerank calls (ER-39).
